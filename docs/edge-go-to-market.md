# angar Edge: partner and MSP go-to-market

**Thesis.** SMEs and mid-market companies in Italy and Germany already pay someone to run their
network. Reach them through those partners. For a partner, angar is a new recurring service
("AI governance") that fits alongside the firewall and backup services they already manage. The
Edge sensor is the wedge: it goes onto a network the partner already controls.

## 1. Target partners

| Segment | Italy | Germany | Why they buy |
|---|---|---|---|
| MSPs / managed IT | IT-services firms with 5–50 staff, often regional | IT-Systemhäuser, MSPs in the Bitkom and ISN networks | Recurring revenue, a new NIS2 story, installs on boxes they already run |
| Firewall resellers | Fortinet and Sophos partners, WatchGuard | Same, plus Securepoint and LANCOM | Syslog is already flowing: turning on angar is a 10-minute change |
| System integrators / OT | Integrators for manufacturing districts | Mittelstand integrators | Plant networks, NIS2 "important entities", no desktop agents allowed |
| Accountants and advisers (commercialisti, Steuerberater) | Commercialisti, CAF, labour consultants | Steuerberater, DATEV ecosystem | They own the SME relationship and the cost savings story. They refer clients or run the partner console |

Start with **20 named partners per country**, found through Fortinet and Sophos partner locators,
LinkedIn, and regional Confindustria and IHK events.

## 2. Offer

- **30% off** list price on angar plans and devices (`EDGE.partnerDiscountPct`). The partner
  bills the client, and angar bills the partner monthly.
- **Edge software is free** for partner-managed clients on Growth and above. The device is €20.30
  per month to the partner and €29 at list.
- **Partner console**, free. It lists every client workspace with its spend, savings, Edge fleet
  health, privacy mode and an estimate of the partner's margin. The partner marks each client as
  "managed by me".
- **Suggested bundle, "AI governance as a service":** €290–490 per month per client, set by the
  partner. It covers the angar plan, one Edge sensor or a device, and a monthly review call with
  the report and a 1-page NIS2 / AI Act evidence pack. Typical partner margin is €120–250 per
  client per month.
- Co-selling on the first 3 deals: we join the calls, give a 30-day free pilot, and supply
  demo data.
- Material: a German and an Italian one-pager, a pilot checklist, an info-sheet template for the
  works council, a model Italian "accordo sindacale / informativa art. 4" text, and a
  Betriebsvereinbarung template.

## 3. Pitch by buyer

| Buyer | Pain | Line | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CFO / owner** | AI costs scattered across cards and seats | "We find AI you pay for twice and seats nobody uses. The 90-day guarantee covers the subscription." | Savings page and monthly report |
| **IT / security** | Shadow AI, data leaving the company, NIS2 duties | "See every AI on the network, including servers and scripts on personal API keys. Block the ones you don't approve and point people to the approved one." | Edge fleet, blocked attempts, invisible AI, large uploads |
| **HR / works council (Betriebsrat, RSU)** | Fear of employee surveillance | "No content, ever. Pick per-department (groups of at least 5) or company totals only. It's configured before the first report." | Privacy modes, what it never does, art. 4 / §87 BetrVG templates |
| **Compliance / DPO** | AI Act Article 4 and 26, NIS2 Article 21 | "A live AI register and a tamper-evident evidence pack, ready for an auditor." | Evidence export |

Opening for a partner: *"Your clients are already using AI you don't see. In 10 minutes, on the
firewall you already manage, you can show them, and bill them for it every month."*

## 4. 30-60-90 days

**Days 0–30: prove the motion.**

- Recruit 5 design partners: 3 in Italy (one Fortinet reseller, one MSP, one commercialista
  network) and 2 in Germany.
- Run 10 client pilots using syslog or DNS software mode. No hardware yet.
- Publish the Italian and German one-pagers and the works-council kit. Add the partner terms to
  the website.
- Metric: time from pilot to first AI found is under 1 day, and at least 5 pilots are live.

**Days 31–60: package and repeat.**

- Convert at least 5 pilots to paid, through partners.
- Ship the first 25 hand-built N100 devices (see `edge-hardware.md`) to partners that have no
  server on site.
- Hold a webinar with a Fortinet or Sophos distributor, and one with an association of
  commercialisti.
- Collect 2 case studies: savings in euros, invisible AI found, and a works-council sign-off.
- Metric: 15 partners signed and €5k in new monthly recurring revenue through partners.

**Days 61–90: scale the channel.**

- Launch partner tiers:
  - Registered: 20% off.
  - Certified: 30% off. Requires 3 or more managed clients and one training session.
  - Premier: 35% off, plus leads we pass on. Requires 15 or more clients.
- Add German-speaking sales and support, 1 person or a freelancer.
- Hold a booth at one event per country, such as a security summit or it-sa.
- Metric: 30 partners, 60 managed clients, 100 sensors online, and €15k in new monthly recurring
  revenue through partners.

## 5. What we measure

- Partners with at least one managed client.
- Clients per partner.
- Sensors online as a share of sensors created.
- Days from sign-up to first data.
- Pilot-to-paid conversion.
- Net revenue retention of managed clients.
- Blocked attempts per client, as a proxy for value delivered.
